The AI Cycle

An experimental project in which an AI system plans, produces, publishes and analyzes short videos for our own YouTube channel, and learns from the results.

What this project does

We are testing whether an automated system can improve on its own through a repeated cycle of planning, production, measurement and learning (a PDCA loop). Each video is treated as an experiment: a hypothesis is recorded before publishing, results are measured afterwards, and the lessons are fed back into the next plan.

About our API client

The AI Cycle system is an internal tool. It is used only by the operating team, and only for our own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and no outside users can sign in to it.

The system uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos that we produced to our own channel (YouTube Data API);
  • read the list, metadata and public statistics of our own videos (YouTube Data API);
  • read aggregated performance reports for our own channel, such as views and average view duration (YouTube Analytics API).

All videos are AI-generated and are labeled as altered or synthetic content on YouTube.
